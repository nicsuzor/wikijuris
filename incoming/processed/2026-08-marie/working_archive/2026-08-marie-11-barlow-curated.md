#### A Case Study: Social Media Minimum Age Legislation

In December 2024 the _Online Safety Amendment (Social Media Minimum Age) Act 2024_ (Cth) inserted Part 4A into the _Online Safety Act 2021_ (Cth), requiring age-restricted social media platforms to take reasonable steps to prevent Australians under 16 from holding accounts. The obligation commenced on 10 December 2025.[^barlow_sma] The scheme is described in detail in the [Content Regulation and Online Classification](../content/#social-media-minimum-age) chapter.

The scheme is a useful test of Barlow's claim because of how it is structured. Barlow's argument that the internet is unregulable rested on two features: the decentralised and cross-jurisdictional architecture of the network, and the sheer number of participants. Regulating every contributor would be impracticable, and would raise jurisdictional problems at every step.

The minimum age scheme does not attempt that. It regulates a small number of platform operators rather than a large number of users. This has three consequences that bear directly on Barlow's claim:

- The number of regulated entities is small enough to be enumerated, which makes supervision practicable in a way that regulating users would not be.
- The regulated entities are identifiable legal persons with assets and a commercial interest in continued access to the Australian market, which supplies both a target for enforcement and an incentive to comply.
- Those entities have a determinate location for jurisdictional purposes, even though the network they operate does not.

The scheme is therefore an instance of the pattern discussed elsewhere in this chapter: regulating the internet indirectly, through the intermediaries that structure access to it, rather than directly through its users. The layer at which the network is decentralised is not the layer at which most people encounter it.

That structural point does not settle whether the scheme will work. Compliance is measured by 'reasonable steps' rather than by outcomes, which leaves open how much circumvention is consistent with compliance. The effectiveness of the age assurance technologies on which the scheme depends, and their differential accuracy across groups, is discussed in the [Content Regulation and Online Classification](../content/#age-assurance) chapter.
